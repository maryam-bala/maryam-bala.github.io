Hi, I'm Maryam. I'm not entirely sure what this blog will become, but I figured the best way to find out is to start.

I'm a PhD researcher at the University of Southampton, working on AI for sustainability — 
specifically on how we can use AI to help organisations make better decisions about energy 
use in buildings.

My path here wasn't exactly straight. I've worked across different fields and picked up 
different skills along the way, and at some point it stopped feeling like I was switching 
directions and started feeling like everything was quietly pointing toward the same place. 
Buildings, data, AI, people — it all connects, and that's what excites me most about 
this work.

I'm only at the beginning of this journey, and honestly, I'm still figuring a lot of it out.

This blog will be a space for me to share that journey — research updates, things I'm 
learning, occasional reflections. Nothing too polished. Just honest.

Thanks for stopping by.

— Maryam
