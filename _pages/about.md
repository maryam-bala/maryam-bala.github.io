Hi, I'm Maryam — a PhD researcher at the [SUSTAI Centre for Doctoral Training](https://www.southampton.ac.uk/sustai) at the University of Southampton, where I work at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Sustainability.

My doctoral research focuses on developing a **Decision Support Toolkit for Shared Energy Systems**, designing AI-based tools that help organisations make smarter, data-driven decisions about energy use across groups of buildings. 
Alongside my core research, I have a strong interest in **Natural Language Processing and large language models**, including their applications in structured dialogue, pragmatic reasoning, and human-AI interaction.

- **PhD in AI for Sustainability** — University of Southampton *(in progress)*
- **MSc in Artificial Intelligence** — University of Aberdeen
- **BEng in Architectural Conservation and Sustainability Engineering** — Carleton University

## Research interests

- AI and machine learning for energy systems and the built environment
- Decision support tools for sustainability and urban planning
- Natural language processing and large language models
- Pragmatics, dialogue, and child-directed speech
- Human-AI interaction

## Background

Before starting my PhD, I built experience across research, government, and industry — developing a perspective that bridges technical depth with real-world impact. I'm particularly drawn to interdisciplinary work where AI meets pressing societal challenges, from sustainable energy to language and communication.
